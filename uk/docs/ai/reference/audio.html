audio.detect_beats

Detect beats

СкасовуєтьсяToolbar › BeatMCP audio_detect_beats

Finds the beats/onsets (drum hits, note attacks) of an audio clip or of a video clip's sound, like CapCut's auto Beat feature, and returns their times on the TIMELINE in microseconds (speed and reverse of the segment are taken into account; only beats inside the segment's timeline range are returned). The source range is decoded to mono 11025 Hz and analysed in pure Go: spectral flux of the log-magnitude spectrum (512-sample window, 11.6 ms hop), adaptive threshold (local mean + delta) with peak picking, then each beat is refined in the time domain to the attack sample (typically within a few milliseconds). bpm is a rough tempo from the median beat interval. With add_markers=true a timeline marker labelled "Beat" is added at every beat (existing markers within 10 ms are kept, not duplicated) so clips can be snapped to the music. At most the first 20 minutes of the clip are analysed. Without add_markers the project is unchanged (note: the call is still recorded as an undo step).

segment_idобов’язковийstringID of the target segment (clip). Get it from project.get → tracks[].segments[].id.
add_markersbooleanWhen true, add a timeline marker (label "Beat") at every detected beat.
Типово false

audio.extract

Extract audio

СкасовуєтьсяTimeline › Context menu › Extract audioMCP audio_extract

CapCut's "Extract audio": separates the sound of a video clip into its own audio clip. A new segment with the same material, source range, timeline range, speed (incl. keep_pitch/curve), reverse flag, volume, fades, volume keyframes, voice effect, noise reduction and loudness normalisation is placed on the first unlocked audio track that is free at that time range (a new audio track is created otherwise). The video segment is muted (volume 0, its volume keyframes move to the audio clip) and both segments are linked with a shared link_group so they move/trim together while timeline linkage is on (an existing link_group of the video segment is reused). Works on video clips on the main or overlay tracks whose material has an audio stream; fails with invalid_argument for photos, freeze frames or silent videos, and with conflict when the clip's track is locked or its audio was already extracted.

audio.loudness

Measure loudness

Без кроку скасуванняInspector › Audio › BasicMCP audio_loudness

Measures the loudness of a clip's sound over its source range with ffmpeg's ebur128 filter (EBU R128 / ITU-R BS.1770-4: K-weighted, gated integrated loudness in LUFS, loudness range in LU and true peak in dBFS; mono sources are measured as dual-mono because they play on both channels). Speed, reverse and voice effects are not applied to the measurement. Also reports the clip volume in dB, the gain that "Normalize loudness" applies (brings the clip to target_lufs = -14 LUFS, but never lifts the true peak above -1 dBTP; limited to -30..+20 dB) and effective_lufs = integrated + volume_db (+ normalize gain when enabled). Silence reports -70 LUFS / -144 dBFS. Useful for AI agents balancing music against voice-over: e.g. keep background music ~10-15 LU below speech. Results are cached per file and range.

audio.restore

Restore audio

СкасовуєтьсяTimeline › Context menu › Restore audioMCP audio_restore

Undoes a separation at any later time (CapCut's "Restore audio"): deletes the audio clip that audio.separate / audio.extract created from a video clip and gives the video its sound back. Pass EITHER segment of the pair: the video clip (main/overlay track) or the separated audio clip (same material and link_group on an audio track). The video gets the audio clip's current volume, fades, volume keyframes, voice effect, noise reduction and loudness normalisation, so it sounds as before; trims or moves made to the audio clip alone are dropped (the video plays its own source range). If the separated audio was split, every piece is removed. An audio track left empty is deleted, and the link_group is cleared when no other clip remains linked. Fails with invalid_argument when the clip has no separated audio, and with conflict when one of the tracks is locked.

sfx.add

Add sound effect

СкасовуєтьсяLibrary › Audio › Sound effectsMCP sfx_add

Adds a built-in sound effect to the timeline as an audio clip — the "+" button (or drag) of a sound effect in the Audio panel. The effect's WAV is synthesised once into the library folder (<DataDir>/library/sfx/<id>.wav), imported as a material named after the effect (the same material is reused when the effect is added again) and placed at at_us (default: the playhead) on the first audio track that is free for its whole length; a new audio track is created when all are busy (or next to track_id when that track is busy). The clip keeps the effect's full length; trim, fade or change its volume afterwards with the usual segment commands. Typical use: whoosh on a cut, pop when a sticker appears, ding on a key point, riser that ENDS at a drop (at_us = drop_us - duration_us).

tts.estimate

Estimate speech cost

Без кроку скасуванняLibrary › Audio › Text to speechMCP tts_estimate

Prices a speech before it is made and returns the confirm_token that tts.speak / tts.from_text / tts.provider.test need for PAID cloud providers — show cost_text to the user and ask; an AI agent must always ask before paid speech. Takes the same voice/provider/model/params/rate/language arguments as tts.speak plus the text (or project_id + segment_id of a text clip for tts.from_text). Local and self-hosted voices are free (no token needed); a speech already rendered for the project is free (cached=true). The estimate uses the provider's published prices (as_of date) — subscription plans (ElevenLabs) are billed in characters from the plan's quota instead (quota shows what is left). note warns when the daily spending limit would be exceeded.

Reads a text segment aloud (CapCut's Text → Text to speech): speaks the segment's content with a local or cloud voice (same provider/model/params/confirm_token rules as tts.speak — paid providers need tts.estimate {project_id, segment_id} first) and places the speech as an audio clip starting exactly at the text's start, on the first free audio track. tts.speak

Text to speech

СкасовуєтьсяПлатнаLibrary › Audio › Text to speechMCP tts_speak

Speaks text and adds the speech to the timeline as a new audio clip (CapCut's Text to speech → Add to timeline). The speech is rendered by the selected engine — a local one (Windows SAPI, eSpeak NG, macOS say) or a cloud/self-hosted provider (ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Azure, Google, Edge, Kokoro… — see tts.providers, tts.voices, tts.models; long text is split for providers with short limits and joined), trimmed of leading/trailing silence, loudness-normalised to about -16 LUFS (peaks <= -1.5 dBTP, so voice-overs from every engine sound equally loud) and saved as a 48 kHz WAV in the project's cache (cache/tts/); the file is imported as a material named "Speech: <text>" and placed at at_us on the first free audio track (a new audio track when all are busy, or on track_id). The same text, voice and rate reuse the cached file. Up to 5000 characters per call; for narration split long scripts into sentences or paragraphs. PAID cloud providers need the user's consent: call tts.estimate first, show the cost and pass its confirm_token (invalid_argument field confirm_token otherwise); the charge is recorded in the spending ledger and the daily limit applies (spending.get). Fails with not_available when no engine/provider is usable (the hint explains what to install or configure) and not_found for an unknown voice.
