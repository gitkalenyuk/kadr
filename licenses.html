Licences

Kadr is closed-source freeware built on open-source components. This page summarises the Kadr licence, lists those components and their licences, and covers the assets used on this website.

The Kadr licence

Kadr is distributed under a freeware end-user licence agreement (EULA). In plain words:

  • Free to use. You can install and use Kadr on any number of computers you own or control, for any lawful purpose, including commercial work.
  • Your output is yours. Videos, images, audio and projects you create belong to you. You can publish and sell them without owing anything to Kadr. Rights in media you import from others still apply.
  • Closed source. You may not reverse engineer, modify or redistribute Kadr's programs or installers, for example on download portals. Link to the official download page instead.
  • Automation is welcome. Controlling Kadr through its documented interfaces (the REST API, the MCP server and the command-line tool) is explicitly allowed and is not reverse engineering.
  • No warranty. Kadr is provided as is, free of charge. Keep backups of your media and projects.

This summary is not the licence. The full text is in EULA.txt on this site, and in the licenses folder of every Kadr installation.

FFmpeg

FFmpeg is not included with Kadr. Kadr runs ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe as separate programs. When you agree, Kadr downloads a GPL build of FFmpeg 7.1 for Windows x64 from the BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds project, or uses a copy you already have. That FFmpeg is distributed to you by its authors under its own licence; the GPL builds are licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 or later. It is not part of Kadr and the Kadr EULA doesn't cover it. The source code is available from the FFmpeg project and from the BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds releases. Licence details: ffmpeg.org/legal.html. FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.

Built-in library content

Kadr's transitions, effects, filters, clip and text animations, text templates, caption styles, stickers, sound effects and generated backgrounds are original and are produced by Kadr's own code. None of them are copied from other products. They are part of Kadr and you can use them freely in your videos, including commercial ones.
