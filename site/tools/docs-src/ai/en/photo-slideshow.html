You get a 16:9 slideshow where every photo slowly zooms and drifts, alternating between zooming in and zooming out. Photos blend with 0.8 s dissolves, the music fades in and out, and the title rises in letter by letter.

  • Time: ~2 min
  • You need: A folder of photos and a music track
  • Main commands: media.import, timeline.segment.add, keyframe.add, transition.apply_all, segment.set_fade, text.add, text.set_glyph_animation, preview.layout, export.start

Ask Claude

Make a 16:9 slideshow in Kadr from the photos in C:\Users\you\Pictures\summer, 4 seconds per photo. Give every photo a slow Ken Burns move: alternate zooming in from 110% to 125% and zooming out from 125% to 110%, with a gentle sideways drift and ease-in-out, and make sure no black edges ever show. Use 0.8 s mix transitions between all photos. Put C:\Users\you\Music\warm.mp3 under the whole slideshow with a 1 s fade-in and a 2 s fade-out. Add the title "Summer 2026" from 0.3 s to 3.5 s with the title_bold template, letters rising in and fading out. Show me frames at 0.1 s and 3 s, then export 1080p.

Step by step

Each step shows the same calls twice. The kadrctl tab is for PowerShell: a line that starts with kadrctl --% passes JSON, and without --% the same line also works in Command Prompt. The MCP tab is the tool call an agent sends — the tool name and its arguments. Ids such as prj_…, mat_… and seg_… come from the results of earlier steps, so yours will differ. Paths under C:/Users/you are examples.

    No black edges. transform.x is measured in half canvas widths (±1 puts the clip’s centre on the edge), and scale 1 means the photo fits the canvas. For a photo with the canvas’s shape, at scale s it can move sideways by at most s − 1 before an edge shows: at 1.1 that is 0.1, so a drift of ±0.05 is safe. Photos of another shape (3:2, 4:3) fit by height at scale 1 and leave side bars — multiply the scales by the cover factor (about 1.19 for 3:2 and 1.33 for 4:3 on a 16:9 canvas), or fill the bars with segment.set_background.

Variations

  • Ready-made motion. For a quicker result without keyframes, use the combo_ken_burns_in and combo_ken_burns_out clip animations with animation.set (kind combo).
  • Other transitions. Try luma_fade, light_leak, film_burn_warm or photo_drop.
  • Other title looks. Word-art effects such as fx_gold_metal or fx_sunset_gradient (text.apply_effect), and letter animations such as in_typewriter or in_word_blur; library.list with kind text_animation lists all of them.
  • Vertical slideshow. On a 9:16 canvas landscape photos fit the width; fill the space with segment.set_background (type blur, apply_to_all).
  • One grade for every photo. filter.add over the whole slideshow, for example golden at 60%.

Tips

  • To fit the slides to the song, divide the song’s duration_us by the number of photos and use that as each photo’s duration_us.
  • Keyframes stay attached to the photo when you later trim or move it.
  • While a property has keyframes, segment.set_transform writes a keyframe at the playhead instead of changing the static value — use keyframe.add with an explicit time_us to edit a move.
  • segment.get with time_us shows the interpolated scale and position at any moment.
