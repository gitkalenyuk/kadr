You get a 9-second 16:9 promo in three scenes: a neon headline over a moving grid, the product dropping in next to a gold title, then the price with a “Limited” tag. Whooshes and a ding sit on the cuts, a local voice reads the voice-over, and you get it as MP4 and as a looping GIF — with no stock footage at all.

  • Time: ~3 min
  • You need: A product photo (a transparent PNG works best)
  • Main commands: media.generate, transition.set, timeline.segment.add, animation.set, text.add, text.apply_effect, text.set_glyph_animation, sticker.add, sfx.add, tts.speak, export.start

Ask Claude

Make a 9-second 16:9 promo in Kadr for my headphones, the Aurora X1, using C:\Users\you\Pictures\aurora-x1.png. Three 3-second scenes on generated backgrounds: neon_floor, then candy_flow, then midnight_glow, with a flash_zoom transition and then a cube_turn_left. Scene 1: "NEW DROP" in big neon pink word art, letters popping in. From 3 s, the product on its own track on the right at 60% size, dropping in with a bounce and then floating gently. Scene 2: "Aurora X1" in gold metal on the left, scrambling in. Scene 3: "$129" with the price_tag template plus a tag_limited sticker that pops in. Add a fast whoosh before the first cut, a ding at 6 s and a pop when the sticker appears. Add a voice-over from 0.3 s: "Meet Aurora X1. Thirty hours of battery, zero cables. Now just one twenty-nine." Show me a contact sheet, then export 1080p MP4 and a GIF.

Step by step

Each step shows the same calls twice. The kadrctl tab is for PowerShell: a line that starts with kadrctl --% passes JSON, and without --% the same line also works in Command Prompt. The MCP tab is the tool call an agent sends — the tool name and its arguments. Ids such as prj_…, mat_… and seg_… come from the results of earlier steps, so yours will differ. Paths under C:/Users/you are examples.

  1. Generate three backgrounds

    media.generate renders backgrounds locally and, with add_to_timeline, places them on the main track. Without at_us each one is appended after the previous one. neon_floor and candy_flow are moving MP4s; midnight_glow is a still PNG whose duration_us sets how long it stays on screen. They are rendered at the canvas size, 1920×1080.

    kadrctl run project.create --name "Aurora X1 promo" --ratio 16:9
kadrctl run media.generate --project_id prj_acdc53cc560a --preset_id neon_floor --duration_us 3s --add_to_timeline --at_us 0
kadrctl run media.generate --project_id prj_acdc53cc560a --preset_id candy_flow --duration_us 3s --add_to_timeline
kadrctl run media.generate --project_id prj_acdc53cc560a --preset_id midnight_glow --duration_us 3s --add_to_timeline

Variations

  • Product photo with a background. ai.remove_background cuts out the product locally when rembg or onnxruntime with a matting model is installed — check with ai.capabilities first.
  • Read the headline aloud. tts.from_text speaks a text clip and links the speech to it; with sync_duration the text clip is resized to the speech.
  • Vertical ad. Use 9:16 and stack the elements: product at y −0.3, headline at y 0.45.
  • Reuse the look. presets.save with kind text_style stores the neon headline in My kit; presets.apply puts it on any text in any project.
  • Countdown opener. The countdown_3 preset of media.generate adds a 3-2-1 intro.

Tips

  • library.list with kind text_effect shows the word-art presets, text_animation the letter animations, sticker the stickers and sound_effect the sounds. Each item has a thumbnail URL you can open with the token.
  • For a shorter GIF add range_end_us (for example 3000000) to export only the first scene.
