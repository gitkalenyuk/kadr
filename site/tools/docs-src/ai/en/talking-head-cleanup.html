You get a vertical short made from a raw talking-head take. Background noise is reduced, the voice is levelled, dead air is cut, every “um” and “uh” is gone, and captions show three words at a time. In our test a 26.8 s take became a 15.9 s short.

  • Time: ~2 min
  • You need: A talking-head video (landscape is fine)
  • Main commands: segment.set_audio, segment.set_video_cleanup, ai.auto_reframe, ai.remove_silence, transcript.transcribe, transcript.remove_fillers, transcript.remove_pauses, captions.from_transcript, export.start

Ask Claude

Clean up my talking-head video C:\Users\you\Videos\tips-take3.mp4 in Kadr and turn it into a vertical short. Reduce background noise and normalise the loudness, lightly denoise the picture, and reframe it to 9:16 following me. Cut silences longer than 0.7 s, then transcribe it in English and remove filler words and any pauses over 0.6 s that are left. Show me which fillers you found before cutting them. Add captions with the cap_pill_active style, 3 words at a time. Show me a frame, then export 1080p to C:\Users\you\Videos\Kadr\tips-short.mp4.

Step by step

Each step shows the same calls twice. The kadrctl tab is for PowerShell: a line that starts with kadrctl --% passes JSON, and without --% the same line also works in Command Prompt. The MCP tab is the tool call an agent sends — the tool name and its arguments. Ids such as prj_…, mat_… and seg_… come from the results of earlier steps, so yours will differ. Paths under C:/Users/you are examples.

  1. Create, import and place the take

    Start with a 16:9 project; the reframe step switches the canvas to 9:16.

    kadrctl run project.create --name "Tips short" --ratio 16:9
kadrctl --% run media.import --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --paths "[\"C:/Users/you/Videos/tips-take3.mp4\"]"
kadrctl run timeline.segment.add --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --material_id mat_745ea2a4d7e6
    {"name": "project_create", "arguments": {"name": "Tips short", "ratio": "16:9"}}
{"name": "media_import", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "paths": ["C:/Users/you/Videos/tips-take3.mp4"]}}
{"name": "timeline_segment_add", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "material_id": "mat_745ea2a4d7e6"}}

  2. Clean the sound and the picture first

    Set these before cutting: every piece made by a later cut keeps its clip’s settings, so you set them once. segment.set_audio turns on noise reduction and loudness normalisation; segment.set_video_cleanup applies light denoising (0–1) while the video is decoded.

    kadrctl run segment.set_audio --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --segment_id seg_997808f11662 --reduce_noise --normalize_loudness
kadrctl run segment.set_video_cleanup --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --segment_id seg_997808f11662 --denoise 0.3
    {"name": "segment_set_audio", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "segment_id": "seg_997808f11662", "reduce_noise": true, "normalize_loudness": true}}
{"name": "segment_set_video_cleanup", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "segment_id": "seg_997808f11662", "denoise": 0.3}}

  3. Reframe to 9:16

    ai.auto_reframe with set_canvas switches the project to 9:16 and animates the framing to follow the speaker. Running it before the cuts means one call covers the whole take.

    kadrctl run ai.auto_reframe --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --segment_id seg_997808f11662 --ratio 9:16 --set_canvas
    {"name": "ai_auto_reframe", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "segment_id": "seg_997808f11662", "ratio": "9:16", "set_canvas": true}}

  4. Cut the silences

    ai.remove_silence measures the audio level and cuts every stretch quieter than −40 dBFS that lasts longer than 0.7 s, keeps 120 ms of padding on each side so words are not clipped, and closes the gaps. The result lists what was removed and the ids of the remaining pieces (segment_ids).

    kadrctl run ai.remove_silence --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --segment_id seg_997808f11662 --threshold_db -40 --min_silence_us 0.7s --padding_us 120ms
    {"name": "ai_remove_silence", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "segment_id": "seg_997808f11662", "threshold_db": -40, "min_silence_us": 700000, "padding_us": 120000}}

  5. Transcribe

    transcript.transcribe runs Whisper locally and stores every word with its timing on the material. Kadr maps the words onto the pieces left after the cuts, so the transcript always matches the timeline. Without segment_id it transcribes every audible clip.

    kadrctl run transcript.transcribe --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --language en --wait
    {"name": "transcript_transcribe", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "language": "en", "wait": true}}
    Hi everyone. Um, today I want to show you three quick editing tips. Uh, the first one is simple,
cut your pauses. The second one is, um, add captions to every video. And the third is to keep it
short and punchy. Thanks for watching.

  6. Remove filler words

    transcript.remove_fillers with preview only reports what it found (here Um, Uh, and um,). Without preview it cuts them, and the rest of the timeline moves up to close each gap.

    kadrctl run transcript.remove_fillers --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --preview
kadrctl run transcript.remove_fillers --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0
    {"name": "transcript_remove_fillers", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "preview": true}}
{"name": "transcript_remove_fillers", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0"}}

  7. Tighten the remaining pauses

    transcript.remove_pauses works from word timings rather than the audio level: every gap between words longer than 0.6 s is shortened to 0.25 s. After ai.remove_silence it catches pauses filled with breaths or room noise. In our clean test recording there was nothing left to remove.

    kadrctl run transcript.remove_pauses --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --min_pause_us 0.6s --keep_us 250ms
    {"name": "transcript_remove_pauses", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "min_pause_us": 600000, "keep_us": 250000}}

  8. Captions from the transcript

    The transcript already exists, so captions.from_transcript creates the cues without running speech recognition again.

    kadrctl run captions.from_transcript --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --style_id cap_pill_active --max_words 3
    {"name": "captions_from_transcript", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_ed95831b1bf0", "style_id": "cap_pill_active", "max_words": 3}}

  9. Check and export

    kadrctl run preview.frame --project_id prj_ed95831b1bf0 --time_us 2s --width 360

Variations

  • Cut a phrase by its text. transcript.delete_text with text “thanks for watching” finds the words in the transcript and cuts them out of the video; captions follow the cut.
  • Your own fillers. transcript.remove_fillers accepts extra words such as ["okay", "right so"]. It has built-in lists for English, Ukrainian and Russian.
  • Keep it 16:9. Skip the reframe step for YouTube.
  • Audio only. Export the cleaned voice for a podcast feed: export.start with audio_only and audio_format mp3.

Tips

  • In a noisy room raise threshold_db to −30 so the hum counts as silence; in a very quiet room lower it to −50.
  • Use preview on transcript.remove_fillers and transcript.remove_pauses first. Every cut can be undone, but checking the list is quicker.
  • Transcripts belong to the media, not the timeline. After further edits captions.from_transcript rebuilds the captions without transcribing again.
  • Names spelled wrong? Pass prompt “Kadr, Kyiv, Olena” to transcript.transcribe (add force to redo an existing transcript).
