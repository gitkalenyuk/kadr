Stickers add personality — hearts, arrows, speech bubbles, badges, countdown numbers, and many that move by themselves. Generated media gives you backgrounds, overlays, banners, frames and countdowns without searching the internet. All of them are original artwork made by Kadr.

Add a sticker

  1. Find one

    Open the Stickers tab. There are 394 stickers in 25 categories on the left: Trending, Shapes, Arrows, Emoji, Badges, Bubbles, Callouts, Doodles, Frames, Countdown, Social, Sale, Weather, Sparkles, Animated, Love, Party, Nature, Food, Tech, Retro, Travel, Sports, 3D and Folk (Ukrainian motifs such as a Petrykivka flower, a stork and “Дякую”). Or type in Search.

  2. Add it

    Click + on the sticker (or double-click it) to add it at the playhead for 3 seconds, or drag it to a spot on the timeline. Stickers go on their own sticker track.

  3. Place it

    In the player, drag the sticker where you want it, pull a corner to resize it and use the round handle to rotate it — see editing in the picture. Drag the clip's edges on the timeline to set how long it stays.

A selected sticker has two inspector tabs: Sticker (exact position, scale, rotation, blend mode, opacity and effects) and Animation. A Loop animation such as floating, pulsing or swinging makes a sticker feel alive; In and Out animations make it pop in and out.

Animated stickers

The 73 stickers in the Animated category move on their own: hover a card to see how. When you add one, it arrives with its animation already set — usually a pop-in followed by a loop such as a bounce, a wobble or a heartbeat. Change or remove it in the sticker's Animation tab like any other animation.

Point at something important with an arrow or a doodle circle, then give it a Pop In animation and a sound effect from Audio → Sound effects.

Generated media

The last category of the Stickers tab, Generated, contains 98 backgrounds and clips that Kadr renders for you on the spot:

  • Gradients — smooth colour blends and soft mesh gradients, many slowly moving: aurora, lava lamp, retro plasma;
  • Backgrounds — golden bokeh, city lights, fireflies, snowfall, a starfield, rising embers, floating dust;
  • Solid — black, white, and green or blue screen;
  • Textures and Patterns — paper, kraft, grunge wall, concrete, canvas, denim, marble, watercolour, wood, film grain, TV static; memphis, waves, polka dots, halftone, terrazzo, honeycomb, gingham, grids, checkers, stripes and more;
  • Overlays — light leaks, a film burn, prism streaks, a lens flare and old-film dust, drawn on black;
  • Banners — empty lower thirds, pills, ribbons, a glass panel, a news bar and a brush stroke to put your own text on;
  • Frames — film strip, neon, instant photo, vignette, torn paper, viewfinder, rounded, postage stamp and double-line borders;
  • Countdown — 3, 5 and 10-second countdowns, a film leader, a flip clock, neon, progress-bar, dot and pop timers;
  • Test — colour bars, with or without a test tone.

Cards with a ▶ and a length are moving videos — moving backgrounds loop seamlessly, so you can place them one after another; the others are still images. Hover a moving card to watch it. Click + to add one at the playhead, or drag it onto the main track or above it. Each generated item is also added to your Media tab, so you can reuse it.

Make your own

The box at the top of Generated creates a custom background:

  1. Choose a type under Custom: Gradient, Solid colour, Soft noise, Floating shapes, Stripes, Checker, Grid, Countdown or Colour bars.
  2. Pick the colours (the labels change with the type, for example Background and Shapes).
  3. Set the Length (s) — for a countdown, From (s) — and switch Animated on for movement.
  4. Click Generate. The result is added at the playhead.

Generated backgrounds are perfect behind titles, as an intro card, under a vertical video with empty space, or as a green screen for testing.

Overlays, banners and frames

These go on top of your video: drag the card to the track above a clip.

  • Banners and Frames have a transparent background, so the video shows around and through them. Add a text clip on top of a banner for a lower third.
  • Overlays (light leaks, film burn, lens flare, film dust) are drawn on black. Select the overlay clip and set Video → Basic → Blend to Screen: the black disappears and only the light stays.
