Stickers add personality — hearts, arrows, speech bubbles, badges, countdown numbers. Generated media gives you backgrounds and countdowns without searching the internet. All of them are original artwork made by Kadr.

Add a sticker

  1. Find one

    Open the Stickers tab. Categories on the left: Trending, Shapes, Arrows, Emoji, Badges, Bubbles, Callouts, Doodles, Frames, Countdown, Social, Sale, Weather and Sparkles. Or type in Search.

  2. Add it

    Click + on the sticker (or double-click it) to add it at the playhead for 3 seconds, or drag it to a spot on the timeline. Stickers go on their own sticker track.

  3. Place it

    In the player, drag the sticker where you want it, pull a corner to resize it and use the round handle to rotate it — see editing in the picture. Drag the clip's edges on the timeline to set how long it stays.

A selected sticker has two inspector tabs: Sticker (exact position, scale, rotation, blend mode, opacity and effects) and Animation. A Loop animation such as floating, pulsing or swinging makes a sticker feel alive; In and Out animations make it pop in and out.

Point at something important with an arrow or a doodle circle, then give it a Pop In animation and a sound effect from Audio → Sound effects.

Generated media

The last category of the Stickers tab, Generated, contains 32 backgrounds and clips that Kadr renders for you on the spot:

  • Gradients — smooth colour blends, some slowly moving (Sunset, Ocean, Aurora flow…);
  • Solid — black, white, paper, and green or blue screen;
  • Textures and Patterns — film grain, TV static, smoke, floating shapes, bubbles, confetti, grids, checkers, stripes;
  • Countdown — 3, 5 and 10-second countdowns and a film leader;
  • Test — colour bars, with or without a test tone.

Cards with a ▶ and a length are moving videos; the others are still images. Click + to add one at the playhead, or drag it onto the main track or above it. Each generated item is also added to your Media tab, so you can reuse it.

Make your own

The box at the top of Generated creates a custom background:

  1. Choose a type under Custom: Gradient, Solid colour, Soft noise, Floating shapes, Stripes, Checker, Grid, Countdown or Colour bars.
  2. Pick the colours (the labels change with the type, for example Background and Shapes).
  3. Set the Length (s) — for a countdown, From (s) — and switch Animated on for movement.
  4. Click Generate. The result is added at the playhead.

Generated backgrounds are perfect behind titles, as an intro card, under a vertical video with empty space, or as a green screen for testing.
