Titles, lower thirds, labels and call-outs are all text clips. Start from a template or plain text, then style and animate it in the inspector.

Add text

  1. Move the playhead

    New text starts at the playhead, so first put the playhead where the text should appear.

  2. Pick a template

    Open the Text tab. Under Templates, the Default category has plain white text; the other categories hold 175 ready-made looks — Trending, Basic, Title, Caption, Fancy, Social, Lower third, Neon and Animated, plus Vlog, Quote, Sale, Luxury, Gaming, Love, Travel, Food, Sports, News, Minimal, Impact and Kinetic. Click a card to see its name and description; hover over an animated template to watch it play.

  3. Add it

    Click + on the card or double-click it, or drag it onto the timeline. A text clip appears on a text track (3 seconds long for plain text) and is selected, ready to edit.

Change the words

With the text clip selected, the inspector shows the Text tab. Type your words into the large box at the top of Basic; press Enter for a new line. The change is applied when you click elsewhere.

To change the length of the text on screen, drag the edges of its clip on the timeline. To place it, drag it in the player.

Font, size and colour

The Font section of Text → Basic contains:

  • Font — click the font name to open the font picker: search, filter by style (Sans, Serif, Display, Handwriting, Monospace, Pixel) or Cyrillic, pick one of My fonts, or download a free one from More fonts with a single click. If the font lacks some letters (Cyrillic, other alphabets), a matching system font fills them in automatically. All about fonts: Fonts.
  • Size, Bold, Italic, Underline and left / centre / right alignment.
  • Case — Aa (as typed), AB (capitals), ab (lower case), Ab Cd (title case). Your original words are kept, so you can switch back.
  • Color — a swatch or the colour picker.
  • Character and Line — the spacing between letters and between lines.

Outline, background, shadow and glow

Below the font settings in Text → Basic are sections you switch on with their toggle. Each has its own colour and sliders:

SectionWhat it adds
StrokeAn outline around the letters — makes text readable on busy footage.
Extra outlineSecond and further outlines for a sticker-like look.
BackgroundA box behind the text, with opacity and rounded corners.
Box styleAppears when Background is on: one box per line (ribbon captions), a slant, padding, a border, a drop shadow under the box, a gradient, and Cut-out — letters cut out of the box so the video shows through.
ShadowA drop shadow with opacity, blur, distance and angle.
GlowA soft light around the letters (intensity and radius).
Gradient fillLetters filled with several colours, as a straight (linear) or round (radial) gradient, at any angle.
3D extrudeSolid 3D depth behind the letters, with colour, depth and angle.
CurveBends the line of text into an arc — Bend above zero curves it up, below zero down.

Word art (text effects)

Text effects are complete looks in one click — 114 of them in 16 categories: Gradient, Metal, Glow, 3D, Outline and Retro, plus Luxury (gold, marble, letterpress), Neon, Candy (glossy, puffy balloon letters), Texture (carbon, plaid, halftone), Sticker, Hand-made (doodle, pencil, watercolour), Spooky (slime, drips), Gaming and Cute. Some of them shimmer or twinkle — hover a card to see.

  • For new text: in the Text tab, switch from Templates to Text effects and add a card. You get text in that style.
  • For existing text: select it and open Text → Effects in the inspector. Pick a card, or None to remove the effect.

An effect simply fills in the text settings (gradient, glow, outlines, 3D and the extra looks below), so you can fine-tune it afterwards.

Patterns, bevels, sparkles and more

Below the effect cards in Text → Effects are the building blocks of word art. Switch any of them on and combine them freely:

SectionWhat it adds
PatternA texture painted over the letters — 28 kinds, from stripes, checks, plaid, polka dots and hearts to glitter, holographic foil, rainbow, galaxy, marble, wood, camo and leopard. Use Motion to make it move.
Bevel & embossLights the letters like a raised surface: a sharp chisel or a rounded, puffy shape, with light direction, contrast and gloss.
Inner shadow and Inner glowA shadow or a light inside the letters — for pressed-in, cut-out or neon-tube looks.
Echo stackColourful copies of the text stacked behind it, like a 70s or 80s title, solid or as outlines.
TextureWear (grunge, chalk, stamp, spray, scratch, worn print), Hand-made wobble for a marker look, and Pixels for blocky pixel art.
SparklesLittle star glints on the letters; Twinkle makes them sparkle.
DripsPaint or slime dripping from the letters.
Stroke gradientAn outline in several colours instead of one.

A moving pattern or twinkling sparkles make the text animate on its own — no keyframes needed.

Letter-by-letter animation

Open Text → Animate. Its 137 animations move the letters and words inside the text:

  • In (55) — how the text appears: Typewriter, Letter rise, Karaoke sweep, Decrypt, Split-flap, Terminal, Jelly pop, Rain drop, Glitch in and more.
  • Out (45) — how it disappears: Implode, Erase, Unzip, Burn out…
  • Loop (37) — a movement that repeats while the text is visible, such as a wave, a pulse, a neon buzz or a glitch twitch.

Click a card to apply it, then set its Duration (or Period for loops) below the grid. None removes it.

These combine with the clip animations of the Animation tab (In, Out, Combo, Loop), which move the whole text clip — fades, slides, zooms, bounces.

More text options

  • Text → Transform — exact position, scale, rotation and blending of the text.
  • Text → Speech — reads the text aloud with a computer voice and places the speech under it. See Text to speech.
  • Save to My kit — the bookmark button next to the sub-tabs saves the style so you can apply it to other texts in any project. See My kit.
  • For subtitles that follow speech, use Captions instead of typing them by hand.

Keep important text inside the safe area: turn on safe zones in the player, especially for vertical videos.
