Good sound makes a video. Kadr lets you add music and sound effects, balance volumes, clean up voices and even create a voice-over from text.

Add music and other audio

Kadr has no built-in music library — bring your own tracks. Import MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC or other audio files in the Media tab, then click + on the card or drag it onto the timeline. Audio goes on its own track below the video and can be moved, trimmed and split like any clip.

Only use music you have the right to use. Platforms such as YouTube may mute or block videos with copyrighted songs.

Volume and fades

Select an audio clip — or a video clip and its Audio tab — to see Basic:

  • Volume in decibels (dB): 0 dB is the original level, −6 dB is about half as loud, all the way down to −∞ (silent); up to +20 dB to boost quiet recordings. The diamond next to it lets you animate the volume, for example to lower the music while someone speaks.
  • Fade in / Fade out — the sound rises at the start and dies away at the end, in seconds.
  • Normalize loudness — evens out how loud the clip sounds, to a level that suits social media and YouTube.
  • Reduce noise — removes steady background noise such as hiss, hum or wind.

The circular-arrow button resets these settings. To silence a whole track, use the speaker button at the start of the track.

Voice changer

Change how a voice sounds with 25 voice effects — Chipmunk, Deep, Monster, Robot, Whisper, Echo, Reverb, Underwater, Megaphone, Lo-fi and more.

  • Select the clip and open Voice changer in the inspector (for a video clip it is inside the Audio tab). Click an effect; None turns it off.
  • Or open Audio → Voice effects in the library, select a clip on the timeline and press + on an effect.

Separate the sound from a video

A video clip carries its own sound. To work with the sound separately — trim it differently, move it or replace it — select the video and press Ctrl+Shift+S, or choose Smart tools → Separate audio. The sound becomes its own audio clip, linked to the video so they still move together while Linkage is on.

The right-click menu of a video clip also has Extract audio, which moves the sound to a new audio clip and mutes the video's own sound.

Sound effects

Open the Audio tab of the library; Sound effects is shown first. There are 47 original sounds — whooshes, pops, impacts, UI clicks, risers, magic, camera, countdowns, cartoon sounds and ambience — sorted by the chips at the top. All were synthesised by Kadr and are free to use anywhere.

  • Preview: click a sound, or rest the mouse on it for a moment.
  • Add: click + to place it at the playhead, or drag it to the exact spot on the timeline — a whoosh right on a transition works wonders.

Text to speech (voice-over)

  1. Open Audio → Text to speech in the library.
  2. Type or paste your text (up to 5,000 characters).
  3. Pick a Voice and a Speed (0.5× to 2×).
  4. Click Add to timeline. The speech is placed at the playhead on a free audio track.

Already have a text clip? Select it: the panel then offers Read this clip aloud, and with Fit the text clip to the speech length the text stays on screen exactly as long as it is spoken. The same is available in the inspector under Text → Speech.

Speech is loudness-matched, so it sits well next to your other audio. You can caption it automatically afterwards.

Which voices are available

  • Windows voices — built into Windows and fully offline. To get more languages, open Windows Settings → Time & language → Speech and add voices, then click the refresh arrow next to Voice in Kadr.
  • eSpeak NG — a free voice engine for about 130 languages. If you install it, its voices appear automatically.
  • ElevenLabs — very natural cloud voices in many languages, if you have an ElevenLabs account. See below.

Use ElevenLabs voices

ElevenLabs is an online service, so it needs an API key from your ElevenLabs account (in your ElevenLabs profile, under API keys; it starts with sk_). Kadr stores the key only on your computer.

  1. Open Settings → Voices (Home → Settings, or Menu → Settings… in the editor).
  2. Paste the key into ElevenLabs API key and click Save. Kadr tells you right away whether the key works and how many voices it unlocked.

Alternatively, without opening Settings:

Option A — a key file (no commands needed). Open Settings → About in Kadr and copy the Data folder path. In that folder create a text file named exactly elevenlabs.key (make sure Windows doesn't add .txt to the name), paste your key into it as the only line and save.

Option B — one command. If you installed Kadr with the Add kadrctl and kadr-mcp to PATH option, open PowerShell and run, with your own key:

kadrctl run tts.set_api_key --provider elevenlabs --api_key sk_your_key_here

The answer tells you whether the key works and how many voices it unlocked.

Then, in Audio → Text to speech, click the refresh arrow next to Voice. The ElevenLabs voices appear in their own group of the list, and in Text → Speech too.

With an ElevenLabs voice, the text you speak is sent to ElevenLabs and uses your account's credits. Windows and eSpeak voices never leave your computer. To remove the key, click Remove in Settings → Voices (or delete the elevenlabs.key file).

More audio tools

  • Speed — audio clips have a Speed tab with Keep pitch; see Speed.
  • Remove silence — cuts the quiet parts out of speech; see AI tools.
  • Beat detection and the loudness meter exist as commands (audio.detect_beats drops a marker on every beat of a song, audio.loudness measures LUFS) but have no button yet — ask an AI assistant to run them, then cut to the markers yourself.
