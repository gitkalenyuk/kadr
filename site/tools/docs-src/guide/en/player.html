The player shows your video exactly as it will be exported. It is also an editing surface: you can grab titles, stickers and overlays right in the picture and put them where you want.

Playback

  • Press Space or the round play button to play and pause.
  • ← and → move one frame back or forward — perfect for finding the exact spot for a cut.
  • The time on the left reads hours:minutes:seconds:frames, followed by the total length.
  • The square button at the bottom right shows the player full screen; Esc leaves it.

Preview quality

The button next to Ratio (it shows Full, 1/2 or 1/4) and the … menu at the top right set how sharp the preview is:

  • Full quality — sharpest.
  • Half — faster.
  • Quarter — fastest, for slower computers or very heavy projects.

This only affects the preview. Exports always use full quality. Kadr remembers your choice. If the preview still stutters, create proxies for your largest videos.

Edit right in the picture

Make sure the Move tool (the arrow at the top of the player, V) is active and playback is paused.

To…Do this
Select a clipClick it in the picture. A frame with handles appears. If several clips are stacked on top of each other, Alt+click picks the next one underneath. Shift+click or Ctrl+click adds to the selection.
MoveDrag the clip. It snaps to the centre lines, the edges of the frame and other elements, and guide lines show when it does. Hold Shift to move only horizontally or vertically, Alt to turn snapping off.
ResizeDrag a round corner handle. Hold Shift for 5 % steps.
RotateDrag the circular-arrow handle below the frame. It clicks into place at 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°; hold Shift for 15° steps.
NudgeClick the picture, then use the arrow keys to move the selected clip 1 pixel at a time, or 10 pixels with Shift.

While you drag, a small label shows the position, size or angle. Each drag is a single undo step. Clips on a locked track can't be moved — unlock the track first.

For exact numbers, use the inspector: Video → Basic → Transform (for text: Text → Transform) has sliders for Scale, Position X/Y and Rotate, plus Flip, Flip V, 90° and Crop. The diamonds next to the sliders animate them — see keyframes.

Zoom and pan the canvas

  • The zoom button at the top of the player (it says Fit or a percentage) offers Zoom to fit (Shift+F), 50% (Shift+0), 100% (Shift+1) and 200% (Shift+2).
  • Shift+= and Shift+- zoom in and out step by step; Ctrl or Shift + mouse wheel zooms around the pointer.
  • To look around a zoomed canvas, pick the Hand tool (H) and drag, drag with the middle mouse button, or just turn the mouse wheel. Press V to return to the Move tool.

Safe zones

Social apps cover parts of your video with their own buttons and captions. Click the dashed-square button at the top of the player to show guides:

  • Vertical videos get a Top bar area, a Caption & controls area at the bottom, a Buttons column on the right and the Safe for text area in between. Keep titles and captions inside it.
  • Other shapes get the classic Action safe and Title safe frames.

The guides are only shown in the editor and never appear in the export. They are a neutral approximation, not the exact layout of any particular app.

Overlays, blend modes and masks

A video or photo placed above the main track is an overlay (picture-in-picture). Select it and use the inspector:

  • Video → Basic → Blend — Opacity and 14 blend modes (Darken, Screen, Overlay, Multiply, Lighten, Hard light, Soft light, Linear burn, Color burn, Color dodge, Add, Difference, Exclusion).
  • Video → Mask — hide part of the clip with a Linear, Mirror, Circle, Rectangle, Heart or Star shape. Sliders set the position, rotation, size, Feather (soft edge) and, for rectangles, Round corners. Invert swaps what is hidden and what is shown.
  • Video → Remove BG → Chroma key — removes a green (or any other) screen. Pick the colour, then adjust Intensity and Shadow. To cut out a person without a green screen, see Remove background.
  • Video → Basic also has Reduce image noise (for grainy low-light footage) and Remove flicker (for flickering lights).
