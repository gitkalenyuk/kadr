Every Kadr release with its notes, newest first. Installers, portable zips and checksums are on GitHub Releases. Kadr installs updates by itself — see the FAQ for how updates work.

0.1.0-beta.1 (pre-release)

The first public beta of Kadr: a CapCut-style video editor for Windows where every action is also an API command, available over REST, MCP for AI agents, the kadrctl CLI and WebSocket events.

  • Editor: projects in 10 aspect ratios, a magnetic multi-track timeline with overlay, text, sticker, effect, filter, adjustment and audio tracks, on-canvas editing, speed and speed curves, masks, chroma key, blend modes, keyframes with easing, and undo for every change, whoever made it.
  • Library: 68 transitions, 70 filters, 56 effects, 136 clip animations, 46 text templates, 22 word-art effects, 39 letter animations, 15 caption styles, 82 stickers, 47 sound effects, 25 voice effects and 32 generated backgrounds. All of them are original.
  • Colour: 14 basic adjustments, HSL, curves, .cube LUTs, auto adjust, denoise and deflicker.
  • Captions and audio: offline auto captions with Whisper in about 100 languages, word highlighting, editing by transcript (remove fillers and pauses), subtitle import and export, local text-to-speech, beat detection, loudness metering, noise reduction and audio separation.
  • Smart tools, all local: remove background, split scenes, remove silence and auto reframe.
  • Export: 480p to 4K, 24 to 60 fps, H.264 or HEVC with automatic GPU encoding, GIF, audio-only, and a background export queue.
  • Automation: 183 documented commands over REST with OpenAPI 3.1, atomic batches with dry run, live events, an MCP server whose frame previews come back as images, and kadrctl with human-friendly time values.
  • Install and update: per-user installer with no admin rights needed, a portable zip, FFmpeg downloaded on first launch, and signed automatic updates with a Beta channel.
