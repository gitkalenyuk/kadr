Recipes

Each recipe is a complete job: a prompt you can paste into Claude, then every call the agent makes, in order, as kadrctl and as an MCP tool call. All of them were run end to end against a real Kadr engine — with generated stand-in media — before publishing.

Vertical TikTok from a folder of clipsA folder of clips and a song become a 9:16 video with subject-tracking reframes, whip-pan cuts, word-highlight captions and music at a steady level. · ~3 min Music video cut to the beatKadr finds the beats in your song and every cut lands on one, with flash transitions, a colour grade and a punch-zoom on the drop. · ~2 min Talking-head cleanup to a vertical shortA raw talking-head take becomes a tight 9:16 short with clean audio, no silences or “um”s, and captions. · ~2 min Photo slideshow with Ken Burns motionA folder of photos becomes a slideshow with slow zoom-and-pan on every photo, dissolves, music and an animated title. · ~2 min Podcast clip as an audiogramA podcast audio clip becomes a square video with a moving background, karaoke captions, a pulsing icon and the show’s name. · ~2 min Product promo with word art and voice-overGenerated backgrounds, a product shot, metallic and neon headlines, a price tag, whooshes and a voice-over, exported as MP4 and GIF. · ~3 min One project, three formatsA finished 16:9 edit becomes 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 videos, reframed or blur-filled, with titles that fit each frame, all exported in one go. · ~2 min Let Claude review and fix its own editClaude renders your edit, spots black edges and cut-off titles, measures them, fixes them, checks the fix and undoes anything that looks worse. · ~1 min Translate and re-burn captionsClaude translates your captions, keeps their timing, and you get a burned-in version plus SRT files in both languages. · ~2 min Documentary from public-domain archive footagePublic-domain newsreels from archive.org, Ukrainian ElevenLabs narration, shots timed to the voice, Ken Burns moves, a film-strip look and fixed captions. · ~10 min (mostly downloading) Choose a transition and an effect by looking at themClaude shortlists transitions and effects, actually looks at how each one moves, picks the one that suits your footage and checks it in your edit. · ~1 min

How to use a recipe

  1. Connect an agent — Claude Code, Claude Desktop or another client — and open Kadr so you can watch.
  2. Paste the prompt from “Ask Claude”, with your own paths and wording. The agent works out the calls; the steps show what it will do.
  3. Or run the steps yourself with kadrctl (PowerShell) — copy each id from the previous result.

Every recipe ends with a check (preview.contact_sheet, preview.frame or preview.layout) before exporting. Keep that step when you adapt a recipe — it is how the agent catches its own mistakes. See the review loop.
