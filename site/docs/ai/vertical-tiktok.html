Vertical TikTok from a folder of clips

You get a 1080×1920 video for TikTok, Reels or Shorts. It opens with your talking clip, follows with short b-roll shots, every landscape shot is reframed to fill the vertical screen, the cuts are fast whip pans, captions highlight each word as it is spoken, and your song plays underneath at a steady level.

  • Time: ~3 min
  • You need: A folder of clips (one where you talk) and a music track
  • Main commands: project.create, media.import, timeline.segment.add, ai.auto_reframe, transition.apply_all, captions.auto, segment.set_volume, segment.set_fade, audio.loudness, preview.contact_sheet, export.start

Ask Claude

Make a vertical 9:16 TikTok in Kadr from the clips in C:\Users\you\Videos\tiktok. Start with 01_intro.mp4 in full (that's me talking), then use 2.5-second slices of the other clips, starting 1 second into each. Reframe every clip so it fills the vertical frame and follows the subject. Put 0.3-second whip-pan transitions between all clips. Add word-highlight captions, 3 words at a time, for what I say in the intro. Mute the b-roll's own sound and lay C:\Users\you\Music\beat.mp3 under the whole video about 12 dB down, with a short fade-in and fade-out. Show me a contact sheet, then export 1080p to C:\Users\you\Videos\Kadr\weekend-tiktok.mp4.

Step by step

Each step shows the same calls twice. The kadrctl tab is for PowerShell: a line that starts with kadrctl --% passes JSON, and without --% the same line also works in Command Prompt. The MCP tab is the tool call an agent sends — the tool name and its arguments. Ids such as prj_…, mat_… and seg_… come from the results of earlier steps, so yours will differ. Paths under C:/Users/you are examples.

  1. Create a 9:16 project

    project.create with ratio 9:16 gives a 1080×1920 canvas at 30 fps. The result is the new project; its id is used by every later call.

    kadrctl run project.create --name "Weekend TikTok" --ratio 9:16
    {"name": "project_create", "arguments": {"name": "Weekend TikTok", "ratio": "9:16"}}

  2. Import the folder and the song

    media.import accepts files and folders; a folder imports every media file directly inside it. The result lists each material with its id, type and duration_us. Importing puts nothing on the timeline yet.

    kadrctl --% run media.import --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --paths "[\"C:/Users/you/Videos/tiktok\",\"C:/Users/you/Music/beat.mp3\"]"
    {"name": "media_import", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "paths": ["C:/Users/you/Videos/tiktok", "C:/Users/you/Music/beat.mp3"]}}

  3. Lay out the clips in one step

    Video clips added without at_us go to the end of the magnetic main track, so the call order becomes the edit order. The intro is added whole; each b-roll clip becomes a 2.5 s slice starting 1 s in (source_start_us + duration_us). As a batch, the four adds are one undo step. Save this as tiktok-layout.json:

    [
  {"command": "timeline.segment.add", "args": {"material_id": "mat_708d08b25bae"}},
  {"command": "timeline.segment.add", "args": {"material_id": "mat_fccc03654420", "source_start_us": 1000000, "duration_us": 2500000}},
  {"command": "timeline.segment.add", "args": {"material_id": "mat_9360221234d9", "source_start_us": 1000000, "duration_us": 2500000}},
  {"command": "timeline.segment.add", "args": {"material_id": "mat_312fa58a675e", "source_start_us": 1000000, "duration_us": 2500000}}
]
    kadrctl batch prj_8a3dc0a150b8 tiktok-layout.json
    {"name": "kadr_batch", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "calls": [
  {"command": "timeline.segment.add", "args": {"material_id": "mat_708d08b25bae"}},
  {"command": "timeline.segment.add", "args": {"material_id": "mat_fccc03654420", "source_start_us": 1000000, "duration_us": 2500000}},
  {"command": "timeline.segment.add", "args": {"material_id": "mat_9360221234d9", "source_start_us": 1000000, "duration_us": 2500000}},
  {"command": "timeline.segment.add", "args": {"material_id": "mat_312fa58a675e", "source_start_us": 1000000, "duration_us": 2500000}}
]}}

    Read the result back with timeline.get — a compact tree of tracks and segments with start and end times:

    kadrctl run timeline.get --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8
    {"name": "timeline_get", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8"}}
    main_video  seg_f3a56bc31a8f  01_intro.mp4   0 s     → 7 s
            seg_a01cb913dfa3  02_city.mp4    7 s     → 9.5 s
            seg_4852e1afa8a7  03_beach.mp4   9.5 s   → 12 s
            seg_4712217ee4d0  04_food.mp4    12 s    → 14.5 s

  4. Fill the vertical frame

    ai.auto_reframe scales each landscape clip to fill 9:16 without bars and animates its position (keyframes) to follow the subject, using motion, edges and skin tones. It runs locally, about a second per minute of video. All four in one batch, tiktok-reframe.json:

    [
  {"command": "ai.auto_reframe", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_f3a56bc31a8f", "ratio": "9:16"}},
  {"command": "ai.auto_reframe", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_a01cb913dfa3", "ratio": "9:16"}},
  {"command": "ai.auto_reframe", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_4852e1afa8a7", "ratio": "9:16"}},
  {"command": "ai.auto_reframe", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_4712217ee4d0", "ratio": "9:16"}}
]
    kadrctl batch prj_8a3dc0a150b8 tiktok-reframe.json
    {"name": "kadr_batch", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "calls": [
  {"command": "ai.auto_reframe", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_f3a56bc31a8f", "ratio": "9:16"}},
  {"command": "ai.auto_reframe", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_a01cb913dfa3", "ratio": "9:16"}},
  {"command": "ai.auto_reframe", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_4852e1afa8a7", "ratio": "9:16"}},
  {"command": "ai.auto_reframe", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_4712217ee4d0", "ratio": "9:16"}}
]}}

  5. Add punchy transitions

    transition.apply_all puts the same transition on every cut of the main track. A short whip_pan keeps the pace up. Each transition is stored on the clip to the left of its cut.

    kadrctl run transition.apply_all --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --type whip_pan --duration_us 0.3s
    {"name": "transition_apply_all", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "type": "whip_pan", "duration_us": 300000}}

  6. Caption the talking clip

    captions.auto recognises speech locally with Whisper and creates cues with per-word timing. segment_id limits it to the clip where you talk, so b-roll sound is not transcribed. cap_highlight_word highlights each word as it is spoken; max_words 3 keeps cues short. wait makes the call return when the job is done instead of returning a job id at once.

    kadrctl run captions.auto --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --segment_id seg_f3a56bc31a8f --language en --style_id cap_highlight_word --max_words 3 --wait
    {"name": "captions_auto", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "segment_id": "seg_f3a56bc31a8f", "language": "en", "style_id": "cap_highlight_word", "max_words": 3, "wait": true}}

    The job ends with "state": "done"; the cues are on a new text track named “Captions”.

  7. Set the sound

    Mute the b-roll’s own sound first. segment.set_volume takes decibels; −60 dB is the bottom of the slider (silent). Save as tiktok-mute.json:

    [
  {"command": "segment.set_volume", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_a01cb913dfa3", "volume_db": -60}},
  {"command": "segment.set_volume", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_4852e1afa8a7", "volume_db": -60}},
  {"command": "segment.set_volume", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_4712217ee4d0", "volume_db": -60}}
]
    kadrctl batch prj_8a3dc0a150b8 tiktok-mute.json
    {"name": "kadr_batch", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "calls": [
  {"command": "segment.set_volume", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_a01cb913dfa3", "volume_db": -60}},
  {"command": "segment.set_volume", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_4852e1afa8a7", "volume_db": -60}},
  {"command": "segment.set_volume", "args": {"segment_id": "seg_4712217ee4d0", "volume_db": -60}}
]}}

    Then add the song from 0 s for the length of the video (14.5 s here). It lands on an audio track. Lower it by a fixed 12 dB so it sits under the voice everywhere, and give it short fades:

    kadrctl run timeline.segment.add --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --material_id mat_446985c2caf5 --at_us 0 --duration_us 14.5s
kadrctl run segment.set_volume --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --segment_id seg_5223e6a5f677 --volume_db -12
kadrctl run segment.set_fade --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --segment_id seg_5223e6a5f677 --fade_in_us 0.3s --fade_out_us 1.5s
    {"name": "timeline_segment_add", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "material_id": "mat_446985c2caf5", "at_us": 0, "duration_us": 14500000}}
{"name": "segment_set_volume", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "segment_id": "seg_5223e6a5f677", "volume_db": -12}}
{"name": "segment_set_fade", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "segment_id": "seg_5223e6a5f677", "fade_in_us": 300000, "fade_out_us": 1500000}}

    To check the balance, measure both with audio.loudness. Background music works well 10–15 LU below speech: compare the voice’s integrated_lufs with the music’s effective_lufs, which already includes the −12 dB.

    kadrctl run audio.loudness --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --segment_id seg_f3a56bc31a8f
kadrctl run audio.loudness --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --segment_id seg_5223e6a5f677
    {"name": "audio_loudness", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "segment_id": "seg_f3a56bc31a8f"}}
{"name": "audio_loudness", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "segment_id": "seg_5223e6a5f677"}}

  8. Look at the edit

    preview.contact_sheet renders a grid of frames from the whole video with the export renderer. Through MCP the agent receives it as an image and can spot black bars, cut-off captions or a weak first frame before exporting.

    kadrctl run preview.contact_sheet --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --count 8 --columns 4 --tile_width 180
    {"name": "preview_contact_sheet", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "count": 8, "columns": 4, "tile_width": 180}}

  9. Export 1080p

    export.start renders in the background and returns a job_id at once. 1080p on a 9:16 canvas means 1080×1920 H.264 MP4; Kadr uses a GPU encoder when one works on your PC. Poll export.status until state is done.

    kadrctl run export.start --project_id prj_8a3dc0a150b8 --resolution 1080p --path C:/Users/you/Videos/Kadr/weekend-tiktok.mp4
kadrctl run export.status --job_id exp_ca964467da2a
    {"name": "export_start", "arguments": {"project_id": "prj_8a3dc0a150b8", "resolution": "1080p", "path": "C:/Users/you/Videos/Kadr/weekend-tiktok.mp4"}}
{"name": "export_status", "arguments": {"job_id": "exp_ca964467da2a"}}

Variations

  • Keep the whole picture. Instead of reframing, show each landscape clip in full over a blurred copy of itself: segment.set_background with type blur, blur 0.75 and apply_to_all on any main-track clip.
  • Other caption looks. cap_pop_word shows one word at a time, cap_pill_active puts the active word in a pill, cap_karaoke_bar fills words left to right. Switch at any time with captions.set_style.
  • Sound on the cuts. Add whoosh_fast just before each cut with sfx.add (here at 6.8 s, 9.3 s and 11.8 s).
  • Cut to the music. Slice the b-roll on the song’s beats instead of every 2.5 s — see Music video cut to the beat.
  • Choose the cover. project.set_cover with time_us sets the thumbnail frame.

Tips

  • The first caption job loads the Whisper model and takes longer than later ones. On an NVIDIA GPU the default small model runs about five times faster than real time; model base is faster and slightly less accurate.
  • If captions fail with not_available, run captions.asr_status: its hint says what to install.
  • To reorder later, timeline.segment.move a clip to a new start_us; the magnetic main track closes the gaps.
