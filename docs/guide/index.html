Kadr user guide

Kadr is a free video editor for Windows that works like CapCut: a library of media and effects on the left, a player in the middle, settings on the right and a timeline at the bottom. This guide explains every part of it in plain words — no technical knowledge needed.

Start here

New to Kadr? Read these three pages in order. Together they take about ten minutes.

1. Getting startedDownload, install, set up FFmpeg and create your first project. 2. Interface tourWhat each panel of the editor does, with a numbered picture. 3. Timeline editingCut, trim, move and arrange your clips.

Popular tasks

Add your videos and photosImport files, drag and drop, proxies for big videos. Add a titleText templates, word art and letter-by-letter animation. Pick a fontFree fonts in one click, Cyrillic, your own font files. Make subtitles automaticallyOffline speech recognition, caption styles, SRT files. Music, voice-over and soundVolume, fades, voice effects, text to speech, sound effects. Transitions, effects and colourMake cuts smooth and give your video a look. Natural voicesElevenLabs, OpenAI, Azure, Google and more for your voice-overs. Generate a shot with AIVeo, Kling, Seedance, Wan — straight into a gap on the timeline. Let the AI assistant editAsk in plain words; approve risky steps; undo a whole chat. Save the finished videoExport for TikTok, YouTube or a GIF.

When something goes wrong

The Troubleshooting & FAQ page covers the usual suspects: the Windows SmartScreen warning, a missing FFmpeg, a slow preview and features that need extra software. All keyboard shortcuts are listed on one page.

Want an AI to do the editing for you? Click AI in the top bar and ask the built-in assistant. Every button in Kadr is also a command that outside AI agents such as Claude Code can use too, and you can watch them edit in the window — that is covered in the separate documentation for AI agents.
