videogen.cancel

Cancel AI video job

No undo stepGenerate › CancelMCP videogen_cancel

Stops a queued or running generation: Kadr stops waiting and asks the provider to cancel when it can (fal, Runway, Replicate, BytePlus, DashScope, Higgsfield, Eachlabs…). A job canceled while still queued is refunded in the spending ledger; providers without a cancel endpoint may still finish and bill it (message says so).

Parameters

ParameterTypeDescription
job_idrequiredstringJob id ("vg_…") of an unfinished job.

Returns

Job with state canceled and a message.

Examples

Cancel a job

curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:7777/api/v1/commands/videogen.cancel \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KADR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{"job_id":"vg_1a2b3c4d5e6f"}'
kadrctl run videogen.cancel --job_id vg_1a2b3c4d5e6f
// MCP tool: videogen_cancel
{
  "job_id": "vg_1a2b3c4d5e6f"
}

videogen.estimate

Estimate AI video cost

No undo stepGenerate › EstimateMCP videogen_estimate

Plans a video generation without running it: resolves provider/model, fills duration from the target range and aspect ratio from the canvas (auto_params), checks references and capabilities, picks the endpoint (text, image, first+last frame, references, video edit) and estimates the cost from the provider's published prices (dated; known=false when the provider bills credits or has no public price). Returns a confirm_token that videogen.generate requires for paid generations: show the estimate to the user, ask, and pass the token only after they agree (it is bound to these exact arguments and price, and expires in 15 min). Also reports today's spending against the daily limit (limit.over = generate would be refused by the overall or per-provider daily limit, with the estimate converted into the limit currency), whether a key is configured, and cached=true when an identical request already has a result (generate then reuses it for free unless new_take). Same arguments as videogen.generate.

videogen.generate

Generate AI video

No undo stepPaidGenerate › GenerateMCP videogen_generate

Starts an AI video generation job and returns at once (job_id); the job runs in the background: references are extracted (current frame, clip frames with FFmpeg) and uploaded, the provider is called with key failover, progress is published as videogen.job events, the video is downloaded to Kadr's results cache and — with a range or segment_id — placed into the timeline as one undo step. PAID: when the operation is billable, confirm_token from videogen.estimate is REQUIRED (the user must have agreed to the shown cost) and the daily spending limit is enforced. Agent workflow to fill a gap: videogen.providers (configured_only) → videogen.models {provider} → timeline.get (find the gap) → videogen.estimate {provider, model, prompt, project_id, start_us, duration_us, use_neighbors?} → ask the user → videogen.generate with the same arguments + confirm_token → poll videogen.job until state succeeded/failed (or wait for events); a succeeded job already carries placed (or place_error when the range could not be used), so do not place it again. Identical earlier requests reuse their result for free (new_take forces a new generation). Failures carry the provider's reason and a hint; retry with retry_of and changed arguments.

videogen.job.delete

Delete AI video job

No undo stepDeletes dataGenerate › Delete jobMCP videogen_job_delete

Deletes finished generation jobs from the history (the Generate panel's Jobs list) and, by default, their result videos from Kadr's results cache (<DataDir>/videogen/results) with their poster frames — to free disk space after many takes. Copies already placed on a timeline or imported into Media live in the project folder (<project>/ai-video) and are never touched, so projects keep playing; the spending ledger keeps its entries. A result file another remaining job still uses (the free reuse of an identical request) is kept. Queued or running jobs are refused (cancel them with videogen.cancel first). A failed job with resumable=true may still finish at the provider: after deleting it Kadr can no longer fetch that paid result. All or nothing: an unknown or unfinished id fails the call before anything is removed. Cannot be undone.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDescription
delete_filesbooleanAlso delete the result videos from Kadr's results cache (default true); false keeps the files on disk (listed in kept_files).
Default true
job_idstringId of the finished job to delete ("vg_…").
job_idsstring[]Several finished job ids to delete in one call (with or instead of job_id).

Returns

{deleted: [job ids], count, freed_bytes (disk space freed; a result still hard-linked into a project folder frees nothing), files_deleted, kept_files: [{path, reason}], pending_files (files open elsewhere, e.g. a preview still playing — deleted within seconds), failed: [{id, error}] when a job file could not be removed}.

Examples

Delete a take

videogen.keys.add

Add video provider keys

No undo stepCredentialsSettings › Video generation › KeysUser onlyMCP videogen_keys_add

Stores API keys for a video provider in Kadr's OS-encrypted vault (pool kind "video") — Settings → Video generation → Keys. Paste many at once (one per line, "label: key" or tab-separated, # comments ignored) or import a file (.txt/.env/.csv/.json; a Google service-account .json for Vertex AI). Keys are used in order with automatic failover: on 401/invalid the key is marked invalid, on quota/credits (402) exhausted, on 429 rate_limited, and the next key is tried; a running job always uses the key that started it. Composite keys go in one entry: Kling "ACCESS_KEY:SECRET_KEY", Higgsfield "KEY_ID:KEY_SECRET", Tencent "SecretId:SecretKey". The result never contains the secrets (masked previews only); duplicates are skipped.

videogen.providers

AI video providers

No undo stepSettings › Video generationMCP videogen_providers

Lists the AI video generation providers Kadr can use — Settings → Video generation and the Generate panel. Built-in presets cover direct APIs (Google Gemini API and Vertex AI for Veo 3.1 / Gemini Omni Flash, Runway, Kling, Luma Ray, MiniMax Hailuo, ByteDance Seedance via BytePlus/Volcengine, Alibaba Wan via DashScope, Vidu, PixVerse, Pika, Higgsfield, Lightricks LTX, xAI Grok Imagine, Tencent Hunyuan) and aggregators (fal.ai, Replicate, WaveSpeed, OpenRouter, Kie.ai, PiAPI, GoAPI, AI/ML API, Segmind, Novita, Together, Runware, SiliconFlow, ModelsLab, Eachlabs, Atlas Cloud, DeepInfra), plus custom providers. configured = it has a usable key and every required setting, so it can generate now; keys are counted by status (ok, exhausted, invalid, rate_limited) but never shown. Each entry says what key to paste (key_hint), where to get it (keys_url) and when the preset was checked against the vendor's docs (verified_from_docs, verified_at). Retired providers (OpenAI Sora 2 shut down 2026-09-24, Stability's video API) are hidden unless include_retired.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDescription
configured_onlybooleanOnly providers that can generate now (key + settings).
include_retiredbooleanAlso list providers whose vendor shut the API down.
querystringFilter by text in the id, name, description, model families or tags (spaces and punctuation are ignored: "omniflash" finds "Omni Flash").
with_presetbooleanInclude each provider's full preset definition (request templates, models, pricing).

Returns

{providers: [{id, name, description, builtin, custom, aggregator, experimental, retired, configured, needs_key, key_hint, keys {count, usable, statuses}, settings [{name, type, doc, value}], missing_settings, default_model, models, featured_models, families, capabilities, docs_url, keys_url, pricing_url, verified_from_docs, verified_at, notes, is_default}], default: {provider, model}|null}.

videogen.resume

Resume AI video job

No undo stepGenerate › ResumeMCP videogen_resume

Follows a generation job the provider already accepted again, without submitting or paying for it again. Kadr keeps paid jobs through outages by itself (network down, laptop asleep, provider 5xx/429): the job stays queued/running with lost_contact_at, next_check_at and a "lost contact … checking again" message, and is checked with a growing pause (30 s up to 10 min) for up to 24 h after submission; unfinished jobs also resume automatically when Kadr starts. Call videogen.resume to check such a job right now, or to pick up a job that failed on Kadr's side (resumable=true: still unreachable after that deadline, its key or custom provider was missing, the download or disk failed) once the cause is fixed — the charge was kept for it because the provider may still bill it. Jobs the provider reported as failed, canceled or finished jobs, and jobs that never reached the provider are refused with a hint (use videogen.generate with retry_of for a new, paid take).

Parameters

ParameterTypeDescription
job_idrequiredstringJob id ("vg_…") of a job waiting out an outage or failed with resumable=true.

Returns

Job (see videogen.generate) — queued/running/downloading again with a "checking with … again" message; follow it with videogen.job or videogen.job events.

Examples

Check a job that lost contact again

curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:7777/api/v1/commands/videogen.resume \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KADR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{"job_id":"vg_1a2b3c4d5e6f"}'
kadrctl run videogen.resume --job_id vg_1a2b3c4d5e6f
// MCP tool: videogen_resume
{
  "job_id": "vg_1a2b3c4d5e6f"
}
